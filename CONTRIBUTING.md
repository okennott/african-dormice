# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in this repository.

## Repository Status

This repository is primarily an **archival research repository** associated with a published paper. It is not under active development, and we are not currently accepting pull requests for new features or analyses.

## Bug Reports and Corrections

If you find an error in the scripts, documentation, or data files, please send an email to the published work's corresponding author via the email listed in the paper describing:

- The file where the issue occurs
- What the current behaviour or content is
- What you believe the correct behaviour or content should be
- Any relevant context (operating system, software versions, etc.)

## Reuse and Adaptation

You are welcome to fork this repository and adapt the scripts for your own research under the terms of the [MIT License](LICENSE). If you do so, please:

1. Cite the original paper (see [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff))
2. Note any modifications you have made in your own repository

## Questions

For scientific questions about the methods or results, please refer to the published paper or contact the corresponding author via the email listed in the paper.
